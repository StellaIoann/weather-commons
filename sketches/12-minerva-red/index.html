Sources and limits

  • Moving dots (Simulate): synthetic, made up for the starter template. They are not dust, wind or any measured flow.
  • The 2024 dust storm over Athens and Libya: exact dates and observations (satellite images, dust forecasts, air quality readings) to add.
  • Deforestation, oil mulching, Esso drilling and Italian colonization in Libya: interpretive threads from Stella's research. Sources, places and dates to check.

What this sketch cannot show: so far, nothing here is observed or modelled data. Dust does not carry a record of the land it came from on its own; linking it to these histories is an interpretation to be argued with sources.
